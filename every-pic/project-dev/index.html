Welcome To College Park

I love checking out this cute neighborhood on my walks. The homes are beautiful and the gardens are lush. The big trees on the street create and archway of shade making it a perfect stroll on a warm day.

Welcome To Mabel's Market

This market sells specialty goods like tinned fish, fun chocolate bars, crackers, and jams. Although my favorite part of the store is the people. Shelly the owner is the sweetest women in Davis and is a ray of sunshine.

Welcome To Mercado Davis

I was used to being spoiled back at home having all the ingrediants I needed in town. Although, coming to Davis there was a lack of Mexican ingrediants. When ever I wanted to make some salsa I had to go to Woodland. When the Mercado opened up my friends and I were happy to cook up all the favorites more frequently.
