Welcome
To Temple

I am maybe a little addicted to coffee and there are alot of options in Davis but I enjoy Temple the most. The interior is warm with the bright woods and the baristas are friendly, making the cafe feel cozy. The ordering process feels like an organic conversation without the register first interraction at most coffee shops. The drinks are always great and the seasonal offerings are unlike anything else out there.
